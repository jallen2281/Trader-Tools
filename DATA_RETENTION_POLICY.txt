--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This policy defines how long each category of data held by the Trader Tools
application is retained, how it is disposed of at end of life, and how those
periods are enforced.

It covers all data held by the application: the PostgreSQL production database,
uploaded document files stored within that database, automated database backups,
and application server logs.

The governing principle is that data is retained only as long as it serves the
purpose it was collected for, and that the person the data describes can remove
it at any time.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The application is operated by a single individual, who is responsible for
maintaining this policy, verifying that automated enforcement is running as
described, executing any manual disposal steps, and performing the periodic
review defined in Section 10.

There are no employees, contractors, or other parties with access to production
systems or stored data.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. DISPOSAL METHODS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Database records are disposed of by hard DELETE, removing the row rather than
flagging it. Uploaded documents are stored as binary columns within the database
rather than on a filesystem, so deleting the record disposes of the file in the
same operation; there is no separate file store requiring a second wipe.

Database backups are disposed of by file deletion from the backup volume during
the scheduled backup run.

No storage media leaves the operator's control, so no media sanitization or
certificate-of-destruction process applies.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. ENFORCEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Retention periods are enforced by automated scheduled jobs, not by manual
action. Section 12 records the current implementation status of each control.

  - Backup retention is enforced by the prune step of the nightly backup
    CronJob, which runs daily at 02:00 UTC and deletes any dump older than the
    retention window.

  - Account purge, cache expiry, and session expiry are enforced by a scheduled
    retention job that identifies records past their retention period and
    deletes them.

  - User-initiated deletions are enforced synchronously by the application at
    the time of the request.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. USER-INITIATED DELETION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Users may delete individual records, individual uploaded documents, or their
entire account at any time from within the application. Record and document
deletion takes effect immediately.

Account deletion scrubs personal details from the active record and disables
sign-in immediately. For 30 days the account may be restored by signing in
again, after which it is permanently purged and cannot be recovered.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. BACKUP RETENTION AND ITS LIMITS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Deleted data may persist in database backups for up to 30 days after deletion,
until every backup created before the deletion has aged out of the retention
window.

Backups are not selectively edited to remove individual records. Doing so would
compromise backup integrity and the ability to restore a consistent database
state. The 30-day backup window is therefore the effective maximum lag between a
deletion request and the total disappearance of that data from all systems. This
limit is disclosed to users in the privacy policy.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. THIRD-PARTY PROCESSORS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Data transmitted to third-party processors is retained according to those
providers' own policies, which are outside the operator's direct control. The
processors are named in the privacy policy. Deleting data from this application
does not retroactively delete copies held by a processor that received it.

To limit this exposure, only the minimum data necessary is transmitted: analysis
requests send summarized figures rather than full records, and document
extraction is a user-initiated action that can be declined in favor of manual
entry.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. EXCEPTIONS AND LEGAL HOLD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Retention periods may be suspended where a longer period is required by law,
regulation, or a legal proceeding. Any such hold is documented in Section 13
with its scope, reason, and start date, and is lifted as soon as the requirement
ends. No holds are in effect as of this version.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10. POLICY REVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This policy is reviewed at least annually, and additionally whenever any of the
following occurs:

  - A new category of personal data begins to be collected.
  - A new third-party processor begins receiving data.
  - A change is made to backup schedule or retention.
  - A change in applicable law affects the periods defined above.

Each review verifies that the automated enforcement jobs are running and
succeeding, that the retention periods above still match what the system
actually does, and that the data category list is complete. The outcome is
recorded in Section 13, including reviews that result in no change.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
11. APPLICABLE LAW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The operator is responsible for determining which data protection laws apply to
this application based on its user base and their locations, and for adjusting
the periods above accordingly. This document defines the retention and disposal
practice; it does not itself constitute a determination of, or certification of
compliance with, any specific statutory regime.
